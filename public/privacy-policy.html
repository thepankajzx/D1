Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26/10/2026

Introduction

Welcome to Definite Habit Tracker ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us at support@thedefinite.one.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information provided by you: We collect email addresses, names, and authentication data (via Google OAuth) when you register.
  • Habit and Usage Data: We store your tracked habits, daily logs, streaks, and application usage data to provide our core analytics features.
  • Device Data: We may collect device information necessary for cross-platform synchronization (via Capacitor/Firebase).

How We Keep Your Information Safe

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures (including strict Google Firestore Security Rules) designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. Your data is scoped strictly to your authenticated User ID, and historical data integrity is maintained through read-only access controls where appropriate.

Sharing Your Information

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Account and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated habit tracking data directly inside the app by going to My Profile > Delete Account & Data. You can also request complete deletion of your account and data by emailing us at support@thedefinite.one with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Upon request, all your personal data and habit records will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

Contact Us
